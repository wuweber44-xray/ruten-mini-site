Ruten Medical - Hemostatic & Tactical Medical Source Factory

Ruten Medical Co., Ltd., founded in 2011 in Guangzhou, China, is a source factory for hemostatic gauze, chitosan dressings, zeolite dressings and vented chest seals. ISO 13485 certified, CE (EU MDR), FDA establishment registration and listing. 6,000 sqm ISO Class 8 cleanroom, annual capacity 30 million units. OEM/ODM, standard MOQ 500 units.

Products

  • Kaolin hemostatic gauze and dressings (EarthShield)
  • Chitosan dressings, sponges and powder (BioShield)
  • Zeolite dressings, gauze and cotton balls (VolcanoShield)
  • Vented chest seals and IFAK supplies
